06 / Security Architecture Role boundaries
Role boundaries protect each building’s data.
Primary Operator
Building Manager
  • Delegated Owner-equivalent operational access
  • Full control over assigned building workspaces
  • Management of rooms, tenants, and collections
  • Access to audit history and export tools
Stakeholder
Building Owner
  • Access limited to linked-building profiles
  • High-level approved financial summaries
  • Visibility into monthly profit and occupancy
  • No access to granular tenant management
End User
Tenant Portal
  • Scoped to individual room and rent data
  • Electricity bill and shared charge visibility
  • Payment guidance and receipt submission
  • Direct contact with Building Manager
Enforced via PostgreSQL RLS and tRPC procedure-level scope validation
